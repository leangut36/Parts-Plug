Privacy & legal

Terms and Conditions

PartsPlug is a marketplace for automotive parts. You must be legally able to enter into agreements and provide accurate account and listing information.

Do not list stolen, counterfeit, recalled, illegal or unsafe products, or misrepresent condition, fitment or ownership. Sellers are responsible for accurate descriptions and lawful sales. Buyers should verify compatibility and condition before purchase. PartsPlug does not currently process payments or provide escrow, shipping, warranties or buyer protection. Arrange transactions carefully and comply with applicable laws, including emissions and road-use requirements.

Do not harass users, spam, scrape personal data or misuse the service. Only upload content you have permission to share. Listings or accounts may be restricted for misuse. Nothing here limits rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

Privacy Policy

PartsPlug uses account email and authentication identifiers, listings and photos, saved items and garage information to provide marketplace features. Public listings and listing photos can be seen by visitors; garage records and favorites are intended to be private. Supabase provides authentication, database and photo storage; GitHub Pages hosts the site. Google receives authentication requests if you choose Google sign-in once enabled. VIN lookup sends the entered VIN to NHTSA; optional photo scanning loads an OCR library from a third-party CDN.

Browser storage retains sign-in sessions, preferences and cached app resources. Hosting and service providers may process technical request information. Avoid uploading personal information in public photos. You can remove your own listings and garage records using available controls. Account deletion and formal privacy requests require an operator support process that is not yet published.

Cookie Policy

PartsPlug uses essential browser storage for authentication, appearance preferences and app caching. These are needed for the selected features. Third-party authentication providers may use their own cookies. PartsPlug does not currently include optional advertising or analytics trackers. Your preference below is stored locally; it does not control cookies on third-party websites.

Safety tips

Verify part numbers, condition and compatibility. Meet in a safe public location and avoid sending money to unknown sellers before verifying the item.
